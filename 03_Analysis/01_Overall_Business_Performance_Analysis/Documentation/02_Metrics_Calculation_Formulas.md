## 1. Total Revenue :

- Σ [Quantity × Unit Selling Price × (1 − Discount %)]

<br/>

## 2. Gross Profit :

- Total Revenue − Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
- Σ [(Quantity × Selling Price × (1 − Discount %))] − Σ (Quantity × Cost Price)

<br/>

## 3. Gross Profit Margin (%) :

- Gross profit / Total Revenue \* 100
- [(Total Revenue − COGS) ÷ Total Revenue] × 100

<br/>

## 4. Net Profit :

- Σ [(Quantity × Selling Price × (1 − Discount %))]
  −
  Σ (Quantity × Cost Price)
  −
  Σ Operating Expenses

<br/>

## 5. Net Profit Margin (%) :

- [(Total Revenue − COGS − Operating Expenses)
  ÷ Total Revenue] × 100

<br/>

## 6. Total Orders :

- COUNT(Order ID)

<br/>

## 7. Total Customers :

- COUNT(DISTINCT Customer ID)

<br/>

## 8. Average Order Value :

- Σ [Quantity × Selling Price × (1 − Discount %)]
  ÷
  COUNT(DISTINCT Order ID)
