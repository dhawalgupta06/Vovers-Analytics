# Vovers Business & Finance Analytics

An end-to-end business analytics portfolio project built around **Vovers**, a fictional omnichannel consumer electronics retailer operating across India.

The project simulates how a business analyst can move from understanding a business and designing its operational database to analysing business performance using SQL and Python and presenting decision-ready information through Power BI.

---

## Project Overview

Vovers is a fictional mid-sized consumer electronics retailer headquartered in **Gurugram, India**.

The company operates through an omnichannel business model that includes physical retail stores, online channels, marketplaces, corporate sales, and B2B operations.

The project was developed as a complete analytics environment covering:

- Business understanding
- Database design
- Synthetic data generation
- SQL-based business analysis
- Python customer analytics
- KPI development
- Power BI dashboard development
- Business performance analysis

The objective was not simply to practise individual tools, but to build a connected analytics project that reflects how multiple technologies can be used together to analyse a business.

---

## Business Context

Vovers operates across multiple regions in India and sells consumer electronics through physical and digital sales channels.

The simulated business includes:

- **120 retail stores**
- **12 warehouses**
- **250 products**
- Multiple product variants
- 10 major product categories
- Multiple suppliers
- Customer and order transactions
- Store and warehouse inventory
- Procurement activity
- Product returns
- Employee and payroll data

Product pricing, sales, inventory, and procurement are primarily analysed at the **product variant level**, allowing different configurations of the same product to be tracked independently.

---

## Project Objectives

The project was designed to answer practical business questions such as:

- How is the overall business performing?
- How are revenue, gross profit, orders, and customer activity changing over time?
- Who are the most valuable and loyal customers?
- Which products, categories, and brands contribute most to business performance?
- Which stores, cities, states, and regions perform best?
- Is inventory aligned with recent customer demand?
- Which suppliers contribute most to procurement activity?
- Are procurement levels aligned with sales and current inventory?
- Which products experience higher return activity?
- How is the workforce distributed across departments, roles, and locations?
