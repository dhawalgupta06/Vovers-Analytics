# Vovers ERP Analytics & Portfolio Project

<br/>
<br/>

# Project Vision

The Vovers ERP Analytics Project is designed to simulate the work of a Business & Finance Analyst in a real consumer electronics retail company.

Instead of creating isolated dashboards or SQL reports, the project is divided into multiple business engagements. Each engagement focuses on solving a real business problem faced by management and demonstrates a combination of technical, analytical, financial, and business skills.

Each engagement will integrate:

- SQL Analytics
- Python (Pandas & EDA)
- Power BI Dashboarding
- Business Analysis
- Financial Analysis
- Executive Reporting
- Business Recommendations

Together, these engagements provide a complete end-to-end analytics portfolio suitable for internship and entry-level analyst roles.

# Engagement 1 : Executive Business Performance Review

## Business Problem

Management lacks a single, consolidated view of the company's overall performance. Decision-makers need an executive summary that highlights the current health of the business and identifies areas requiring immediate attention.

## Objective

Provide a high-level overview of Vovers' business performance through executive KPIs and trend analysis.

## Scope

- Revenue Overview
- Profit Overview
- Order Trends
- Customer Growth
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Monthly Performance
- Regional Performance
- Executive KPI Scorecard
- Overall Financial Health

## Key Business Questions

- Is the company growing?
- Are profits increasing?
- Which regions perform best?
- What are the current business strengths and weaknesses?
- Which KPIs require immediate attention?

# Engagement 8 : Business & Financial Modeling

## Business Problem

Management requires more than historical analysis to make informed decisions. Before implementing strategic initiatives, they need financial models that estimate future performance, evaluate alternative scenarios, and quantify the financial impact of key business decisions.

## Objective

Develop business and financial models that transform analytical insights into actionable decision-support tools for forecasting, planning, budgeting, pricing, investment evaluation, and strategic decision-making.

## Scope

### Forecasting & Planning

- Revenue Forecasting
- Sales Forecasting
- Budget vs Actual Analysis
- Financial Planning
- KPI Forecasting

### Financial Modeling

- Scenario Analysis
- Sensitivity Analysis
- Product Pricing Model
- Product Profitability Model
- Inventory Investment Model
- Working Capital Model
- Store Expansion Feasibility Model
- Profit Improvement Model
- Returns Reduction Impact Model

## Key Business Questions

- What revenue can the business expect in the coming months?
- How do actual results compare with the planned budget?
- How would changes in pricing, discounts, or costs affect profitability?
- How much working capital is tied up in inventory?
- Is opening a new store financially viable?
- Which business initiatives would generate the highest financial return?
- What are the financial implications of different strategic scenarios?
- Which recommendations should management prioritize based on financial impact?

# Final Outcome

By completing all engagements, the Vovers ERP Analytics Project will represent a complete business analytics portfolio that demonstrates the ability to:

- Understand a business problem.
- Extract and analyze data using SQL.
- Perform advanced analytics using Python.
- Build executive dashboards in Power BI.
- Interpret analytical results from a business and financial perspective.
- Present insights and recommendations suitable for executive decision-making.

The project aims to simulate the responsibilities of a Business Analyst, Finance Analyst, or Data Analyst working in a real-world retail organization.
